# Stokvel Digital Trust Ledger

> **Live Application**: [https://bongamanzini.github.io/stokvel-digital-ledger/](https://bongamanzini.github.io/stokvel-digital-ledger/)

An interactive community savings club (Stokvel) accounting ledger and financial transparency system designed to digitize South Africa's R50-billion informal communal savings sector.

---

## The Informatics & Financial Inclusion Angle

- **The Problem**: Traditional Stokvels manage millions of Rands in member contributions using handwritten paper notebooks. Records are vulnerable to physical loss, damage, theft, and disputed verbal agreements between members and treasurers.
- **The Informatics Solution**: A transparent digital trust platform offering:
  - Immutable contribution logging with bank transaction references (FNB, Capitec, Standard Bank, Nedbank).
  - Rotational payout scheduling to ensure fair, transparent distributions.
  - Multi-member verification preventing single-treasurer embezzlement.
  - Instant digital audit trails exportable for annual general meetings.

---

## ️ Features

- **Live Contribution Dashboard**: Real-time tracking of monthly member payments and pending flags.
- **Automated Rotational Payout Engine**: Coordinates monthly capital distributions across club members.
- **Cryptographic Audit Trail**: Timestamped transaction log safeguarding collective community capital.
- **Mobile-Responsive & Low-Bandwidth UI**: Designed for effortless mobile bookkeeping on South African cellular networks.
