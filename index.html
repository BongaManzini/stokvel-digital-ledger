Digitizing Social Capital & Trust

South African Stokvels represent over R50 billion in collective community capital across 11 million citizens. Physical paper records frequently suffer water damage, lost receipts, or disputable verbal agreements. By providing multi-member cryptographic confirmations and automated WhatsApp receipting, digital ledgers insulate community trust from human error.
